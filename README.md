# TOEFL Trainer

Practice the TOEFL iBT on your Windows PC. You can take a full test, practice one skill, and listen back to your speaking answers.

## Open the app

1. On this GitHub page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. In **Downloads**, right-click the ZIP, choose **Extract All**, then **Extract**.
3. Open the extracted folder. Go inside until you see **Start TOEFL Trainer.cmd** and double-click it. Windows may show the name as **Start TOEFL Trainer**.
4. Keep the window open. The first time, it downloads the app tools and English voices. When your browser opens, choose **Start a full mock** or select a skill for a shorter session.

Use a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC with an Intel or AMD processor. You need internet for the first setup. If the download stops, reconnect and double-click the same file again.

## The next time you practice

Open **Start TOEFL Trainer** again. Tests, recordings, and history work offline. For speaking, allow microphone access when your browser asks. When you finish, close the launcher window.

**Want a score for your writing or speaking?** Open **Settings → AI trainer** and connect your account. After a test, click **Assess results**. The trainer scores your responses and decides whether to prepare more practice. To ask why an answer received its score, select it and write in **Discuss this attempt**.
